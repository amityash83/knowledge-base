# Free AI Courses

## Summary
Curated list of free AI learning resources from major technology vendors and education platforms, for structured upskilling across foundational AI, applied tooling, and platform-specific training.

## Details

### Approach
Start with a broad course for fundamentals, then follow with provider-specific training for implementation depth. Convert useful takeaways into durable notes under [[artificial-intelligence]] or related concept pages after finishing a course, rather than leaving them as bookmarks.
